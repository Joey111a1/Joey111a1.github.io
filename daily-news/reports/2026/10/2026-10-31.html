Russia continues efforts to damage confidence in U.S. politics, voting systems and Democrats, including with AI-assisted fabricated videos. China has concentrated more on local contests and policy influence. These activities may intensify distrust, but they are not evidence that votes were changed.

Legal limits and the unresolved risk

States administer U.S. elections, and legal experts said a president could not take control simply by issuing an order. Federal agents would need a warrant and probable-cause showing to obtain election equipment or ballots; ballots would remain under state control even if officials secured copies or images.

The White House said Trump was focused on prosecuting election crimes and improving election security. Himes and Senator Mark Warner said they would seek the underlying intelligence if Trump made new claims. The immediate concern described by lawmakers is therefore an effort to weaken confidence in the result, rather than a documented foreign operation that has altered votes.
